# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, use Artificial Intelligence, sensors, cameras, and computing systems to perform driving tasks with reduced or no continuous human input.

The major AI technologies used in self-driving cars are:

Computer Vision – Cameras capture images of roads, vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, and lane markings.
Machine Learning – Enables the vehicle to identify objects and learn patterns from large amounts of driving data.
Sensor Fusion – Combines information from cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and other sensors to create a better understanding of the surroundings.
Object Detection and Recognition – Identifies cars, pedestrians, cyclists, traffic signals, obstacles, and road signs.
Path Planning – AI determines a suitable and safe route for the vehicle.
Decision Making – The AI system decides actions such as braking, accelerating, changing lanes, or turning.
Control Systems – Converts AI decisions into actions such as steering, braking, and acceleration
# working principle
The basic operation can be represented as:

Sensors → Perception → AI Processing → Decision Making → Path Planning → Vehicle Control

Sensors: Cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and ultrasonic sensors collect information.
Perception: AI identifies roads, vehicles, pedestrians, signs, and obstacles.
Processing: Machine-learning models analyze the collected information.
Decision Making: The system determines what action should be taken.
Path Planning: A safe path is selected based on traffic and road conditions.
Vehicle Control: Steering, braking, and acceleration systems execute the selected action.
Case Study

A practical example is Waymo's autonomous driving technology, which combines cameras, LiDAR, radar, maps, and AI-based perception and planning to navigate roads. Other companies and research programs also use AI-based autonomous-driving systems to improve vehicle perception and decision-making.


# RESULT
Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. It was understood that AI technologies such as Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Sensor Fusion, Object Detection, Path Planning, and Decision Making enable autonomous vehicles to perceive their surroundings, make driving decisions, and control the vehicle with reduced human intervention
